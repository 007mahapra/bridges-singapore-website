Planning Notes

5h / 8h costs

Added for Tanglin, Woodleigh and SingPost because their venue rental has an hourly/subsequent-hour structure. Common Ground and SPH are not extrapolated because a comparable hourly auditorium rental was not supplied.

Capacity

Common Ground's supplied quotation is specifically for 126-pax theatre seating. The 150 and 200 pax scenarios are therefore not estimated.

AV / PA

Tanglin includes a projector but no PA system. SingPost's published material lists microphones, sound system, projector/screen and technical support.

Refreshments

The model assumes very light refreshments at S$7 per attendee. Catering GST, delivery or service fees should be confirmed.

Deposits

Refundable deposits are excluded from Total Cost, but should be included in the event cash-flow plan.

Availability

Tanglin is the only venue explicitly stated as available in the supplied information. Confirm the date, setup time, teardown time and cancellation terms with every venue.

SingPost note: Its published auditorium schedule states Saturday/Sunday/PH pricing of S$860 for the first 3 hours and S$300 per subsequent hour, with rates exclusive of GST.
