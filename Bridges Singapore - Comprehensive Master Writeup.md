# Bridges Singapore: Master Document Writeup

## 1\. Organisational History & Foundation

Bridges Singapore was established in  **2015** , as formally recorded in its inaugural newsletter and founding events. The organisation is a registered society operating under the official Unique Entity Number (UEN)  **T15SS0110K** .The society functions under the motto " **Building Relationships, Inspiring Dynamism and Generating Empathy Society** " (BRIDGES). Its core mission is to facilitate meaningful interaction, communication, and goodwill across Singapore’s diverse cultural and racial landscape. From a strategic perspective, the society focuses on promoting social integration and community resilience, particularly by assisting foreigners in navigating their orientation into Singaporean society. By fostering a spirit of volunteerism and mutual understanding, Bridges Singapore seeks to build a more cohesive and inclusive community.

## 5\. Social Impact and Community Initiatives

Bridges Singapore maintains a robust track record of high-impact report summaries, demonstrating a commitment to both local support and international humanitarian aid.

* **Oxygen Concentrator Drive** : During the COVID-19 crisis in India, the society utilised the  **GoGetFunding**  platform as a strategic tool for cross-border crisis intervention. The drive successfully dispatched  **104**  units (reaching a total of  **150**  units) to alleviate medical shortages and save lives.  
* **Migrant Workers Centre (MWC) Support** : Following an official appreciation letter dated  **17 July 2020** , Bridges was recognised for its support during the Circuit Breaker. The society donated over 100,000 items, including dry food, clothing, and hygiene products, benefiting approximately  **370,000**  workers in dormitories.  
* **Internet & Personal Data Security Awareness** : In  **July 2021** , the society executed a series of free online courses to educate the public on securing personal data and passwords, addressing modern digital vulnerabilities.  
  * **National Day Gratitude Card Project** : A collaborative initiative in support of  \#SGTogether, creating "Thank You" cards for frontline workers.  
* **Project Amrit (Beach Cleanup)** : A significant environmental initiative conducted on  **21 February 2026**  by dedicated volunteers.   
* **Youth Mentorship Programme** : Led by professional counsellor  **Ms. Navroop Sood** , this programme targets the 12–20 age demographic. The inaugural webinar, titled "The Road Ahead," was held on  **8 August 2020** , providing critical guidance and counseling to help youths navigate future challenges.  
* **Beach Cleaning Efforts** : Regular environmental stewardship activities are conducted at  **East Coast Park** , often in partnership with the Lions Club. This commitment was recently highlighted by the  **2026 Project Amrit**  initiative.  
* **Old Clothes, Books & Toys Donations** : Bridges manages a recurring collection and recycling programme for used books, clothing, and toys. These items are distributed to  **Foreign Domestic Workers**  and  **Work Permit holders**  to enhance their well-being and integration.  
* **International Yoga Day** : Observed on **20 June 2026**  at  **Marina Barrage** . The programme included a light yoga session from 5:30 PM to 6:30 PM, followed by wellness games from 6:30 PM to 7:00 PM. It attracted close to 60 participants.  
* **Cultural Programs**  
  * **Ehsaas-E-Sukoon** : Held on **1 August 2026**  at  **Sindhu House**  (795 Mountbatten Road) from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM. This devotional jamming experience features contemporary artists and instrumentalists.   
  * **Dandiya Nights** : A major cultural music and dance celebration held in  **September 2025** .
